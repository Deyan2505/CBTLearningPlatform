**Gap closure this turn — SRC-042 registered** (`11_SOURCE_REGISTER.md`, next available ID):
Daniel David, Ph.D. (Professor, Babeș-Bolyai University), **"REBT in the Context of Modern
Psychological Research"** — official Albert Ellis Institute article,
`https://albertellis.org/rebt-in-the-context-of-modern-psychological-research/`. Fetched and read
in full this session. Plays the same role for Ellis/REBT that the Beck Institute pages (SRC-007…
SRC-010) play for Beck — an official institutional source, above general secondary articles,
below a full textbook. Gives an explicit ABC model definition (A/B/C roles + causal relationship),
rational-vs-irrational belief criteria with examples, secondary/meta-consequences, and
unconditional self-acceptance — all previously either missing or resting on a single blog source.
The article does **not** present a formal ABCDE model (no "disputing"/D, no "E") — that stays out
of scope, not invented.

**SRC-006** (Michael R. Edelstein, Ph.D., Psychology Today) — kept as a secondary, corroborating
source for the philosophical-approach/irrational-demands framing and the illustrative
helpful-vs-unhelpful emotion examples (grief vs. anxiety); its claims that are *now* independently
confirmed by SRC-042 (secondary disturbance, unconditional self-acceptance) are treated as
cross-source verified rather than single-source. Its one claim SRC-042 does **not** corroborate
(anger framing) is downgraded to Excluded this turn — single-source opinion, not required by the
approved learning scope, dropped rather than kept as an uncorroborated contrast.

## §2 — Terminology Map

- "ABC модел" — A (активиращо събитие), B (вярвания), C (последствия) — SRC-042's own terms,
  introduced platform-wide for the first time here.
- "Рационални вярвания" / "ирационални вярвания" — REBT's own terms, kept distinct from Beck's
  "основно вярване" (Week 3/12's term) — related concepts across schools, never merged.
  "Demandingness" rendered as "категорична изисквания" (rigid, absolutistic demand); "flexible
  preference" as "гъвкаво предпочитание."
- "Вторично разстройство" / "вторични последствия" (secondary disturbance/consequences),
  "безусловно самоприемане" (unconditional self-acceptance) — REBT-specific, no collision.
- No "ABCDE" / "disputing" terminology — not in SRC-042, not added.
